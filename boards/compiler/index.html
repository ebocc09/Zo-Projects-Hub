How it sweeps
Measured on this fleet: only about one car in ten reports a new fix in any 200 seconds, so sweeping faster than a minute re-reads the same rows. A parked car's fix wanders — one drifted 17 m in 17 minutes standing still — which is what the metres are for. Below about 25 m the board would record that drift as a journey.

A path cannot be filled in backwards. Garage keeps one current position per car and no history — Datatank's GPS history carries heading, elevation and accuracy and no coordinates at all — so forgetting a path forgets it for good.
